Privacy Policy & Text Message Terms

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Who we are

Dwell Well Holdings LLC ("we", "us") owns and manages residential rental homes in the Dallas–Fort Worth area, Texas. This policy explains what information we collect from residents and applicants and how we use it.

Information we collect

We collect the information you give us when you apply for or rent a home, such as your name, phone number, email address, and the details you share when you report a repair or contact us.

How we use it

We use your information only to manage your tenancy: to send rent reminders, respond to repair requests, share updates about your home, and contact you about your lease.

Text messages (SMS)

If you opt in, Dwell Well Holdings will send you text messages about your tenancy, such as rent reminders, repair updates and property notices. To opt in, text JOIN to (817) 687-4880 (see our Text Updates page). Full terms: Text Message Terms & Conditions.

  • Message frequency varies, typically a few messages per month.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at Dwell.Well@outlook.com or (817) 687-4880.
  • Consent to receive texts is not a condition of renting a home from us.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Dwell Well Holdings does not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or text message consent with any third party or affiliate for their marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We share information only with service providers who help us operate (for example, our phone and messaging provider), and only as needed to provide those services, or when required by law.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and keep it only as long as we need it for your tenancy and our legal obligations.

Contact us

Questions about this policy: Dwell.Well@outlook.com or (817) 687-4880.
