Text updates for Dwell Well residents

Current residents of Dwell Well Holdings homes can get text messages about their tenancy: rent due reminders, repair and maintenance updates, and property notices.

Text JOIN to (817) 687-4880

By texting JOIN to (817) 687-4880, you agree to receive recurring text messages from Dwell Well Holdings about your tenancy (rent reminders, repair updates and property notices). Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of renting. See our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

En español: Envíe JOIN al (817) 687-4880 para recibir mensajes de texto de Dwell Well Holdings sobre su alquiler (recordatorios de renta, reparaciones y avisos de la propiedad). La frecuencia varía. Pueden aplicarse tarifas de mensajes y datos. Responda STOP para cancelar y HELP para ayuda. No es un requisito para alquilar.

What happens next

After you text JOIN, you will get one confirmation message: "Dwell Well Holdings: You're subscribed to tenant updates (rent reminders, repairs, property notices). Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."

How to stop

Reply STOP at any time and you will receive no more texts, except one message confirming you have been unsubscribed. You can rejoin by texting JOIN again.
