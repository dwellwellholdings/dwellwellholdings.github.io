Text Message Terms & Conditions

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Program

Dwell Well Holdings Tenant Updates. Dwell Well Holdings LLC sends text messages to its current residential tenants about their tenancy: rent due reminders, repair and maintenance updates, property notices, and replies to tenant questions. No marketing messages are sent.

How to join

Text JOIN to (817) 687-4880. You will receive one confirmation message. By joining, you agree to receive recurring automated text messages from Dwell Well Holdings at the number you texted from. Consent is not a condition of renting a home from us.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies, typically a few messages per month. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan.

How to stop

Reply STOP to (817) 687-4880 at any time to cancel. You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed. After that, you will not receive more texts unless you join again by texting JOIN.

Help

Reply HELP for help, or contact us at Dwell.Well@outlook.com or (817) 687-4880.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy.
